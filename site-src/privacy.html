Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Applies to the Marblume app (Android and iOS), the web version of the game, and this website.

In short: your game progress stays on your device. If you agree, Marblume also sends anonymous game results so we can make the puzzles fairer — never your name, email, contacts or location, and never for advertising. You can switch this off and delete what was sent at any time. We do not sell data. The Android and iPhone apps can show short videos from Google AdMob — only when you choose to watch one — see Ads.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, Marblume saves this only on your device:

  • which levels you have unlocked, your stars and best move counts;
  • your seeds, collected items, badges, daily puzzle results and streak;
  • your profile: the optional name you type, your avatar and ring colour;
  • your settings (sound, haptics, language, appearance, text size and similar).

We cannot see this. Uninstalling the app (or clearing site data for the web version) deletes it, and Settings → Reset progress erases it at any time. Your profile name is never sent anywhere. If you make a backup code (Settings → Back up progress), the code is shown only to you; we never receive it.

Anonymous game stats (optional)

After a few levels the game asks whether you want to share anonymous results. Nothing is sent unless you choose “Share”. If you do, for each level you finish or leave the game sends:

  • the level number and mode, your moves and the level's par, stars, hints, extra tubes and undos, the time taken, whether you solved it, and whether Relaxed play was on;
  • a random code created by the app on your device (so results from one install can be grouped), the app version, the platform (Android, iOS or web) and the game's language.

It does not include your name, email, contacts, location, advertising ID or any other identifier from your device. We use it only to understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to fix problems. It is not used for advertising, not combined with other data, and never sold or shared. You can switch it off in Settings → Privacy, and Settings → Delete my shared stats removes everything your device has sent from our server. Stats are deleted automatically after 24 months.

Short surveys (optional)

Sometimes a short question appears on the game's Home screen, for example how much you enjoy Marblume. Answering is optional and gives no rewards. We store your answer, the app version, platform and language, and — only if you share game stats — the random code above. Survey answers are deleted after 24 months, or with your shared stats when you delete them.

Ads (Android and iPhone apps)

The Marblume apps for Android and iPhone can show short videos from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one: for a hint when your free hints are used up, for an extra tube when your free tube is used, to double the seeds from a win, or for 20 seeds in the Shop. You can always say “Not now” or use seeds instead. There are never ads between levels or while you play, at most 10 videos a day, none in Expert play, and the web version of the game shows no ads.

To show and measure these videos, Google receives information from your device, such as its advertising ID (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google is responsible for this data under Google’s privacy policy for apps that use its services. We do not receive your advertising ID and do not combine it with your game data or anonymous stats.

  • Your choice: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before any ad is loaded. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Ad choices.
  • iPhone: the app asks whether it may track you, as Apple requires. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are not personalised; the game works the same either way.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Suitable for everyone: ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. If you see an ad that seems wrong for this game, use Settings → Report an ad.

Purchases

Marblume has no real-money purchases. Seeds are earned by playing (or by watching an optional video in the apps) and have no money value.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for the optional stats, surveys and videos, and vibration for optional haptic feedback (switch it off in Settings). The Android and iPhone apps also use the advertising ID for Google’s ads, as described above. Links to this policy and to our Support page open in your web browser.

Where data is stored

Shared stats, survey answers and support messages are stored in our Cloudflare account (Cloudflare D1), sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Like any web host, Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service; see Cloudflare’s privacy policy. To prevent abuse we briefly keep a scrambled, anonymous code made from your IP address for one hour; we do not store IP addresses with your stats.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party tracking scripts.

Contact form

If you use the contact form on our Support page, we receive what you choose to send: your message, the topic and device type you pick, and your name and email if you enter them. We also note the country your message came from. Your message and our replies form a private conversation that only you (through your browser, or the private link we give you) and Marblume support can read. It is used only to help you, never sold or shared, and deleted automatically 12 months after the last message (or sooner on request).

Children

Marblume is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to content for general audiences. Anonymous stats stay off unless someone chooses “Share”. We ask children to get a parent’s permission before using the contact form, and we will delete any message from a child on request.

Your choices and rights

You can stop sharing stats and delete them in the game at any time. For anything else — for example to ask what we hold or to delete a message you sent — use the contact form on our Support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the game's in-app policy will be updated too.
