Questions.

How do I play?

Tap a tube to lift its top marble, then tap the tube you want to pour it into. A marble can only go onto the same colour or into an empty tube. Finish when every tube holds a single colour.

How hard is it?

Hard from the start, and harder as you go. Every level has its own setup (5 to 12 colours, tubes of 3 to 7 balls, one to three spare tubes, sometimes hidden balls), so no two levels look or play the same. Each level shows its difficulty: Hard, Very hard, Expert or Master. Every level is still guaranteed solvable, and undo is unlimited.

What can I collect?

Fifty-five items: 19 ball styles (Gems, Neon, Galaxy, Pool…), 13 tube styles (Wood, Ice, Gold, Bamboo…), 4 tube shapes (Round, Jar, Capsule, Flask), 14 game scenes (Meadow, Aurora, Deep space, Coral reef…) and 5 win effects (Petals, Confetti, Hearts, Leaves, Stars). Most are bought with seeds you earn by playing; 11 special ones, like Galaxy, Ruby and Aurora, are earned through achievements. Your collection shows how and when you got each one.

Is it free?

Yes, Marblume is free to play, with no timers and no lives. Seeds you earn by playing unlock collectables and extra hints.

I'm stuck — what now?

Use Undo as much as you like, take a hint (three free each day), or add one extra tube per level. Every level has been solved by computer, so there is always a way through.

Does Marblume collect my data?

Only if you say yes. Your progress stays on your device. If you tap “Share”, the game sends anonymous results (level, moves, hints, time) with a random code, to help us balance the puzzles — never your name, email or location. You can switch it off and delete it in Settings. See the privacy policy.

Android says the APK is from an unknown source.

That is normal for apps installed outside Google Play. Allow your browser to install apps when asked, then open the downloaded file. The app is signed and only asks for internet (for the optional stats and surveys) and vibration.
