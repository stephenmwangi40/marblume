Help & Support

Answers to common questions, and a way to reach us if you need more help.

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a tube to lift its top ball, then tap the tube where it should go. A ball can only land on the same colour or in an empty tube. The level is finished when every tube holds one colour.

A level feels impossible.

Every one of the 10,000 levels has been solved by computer, so there is always a way through. Use Undo freely (it is unlimited), take a hint (three free each day), or add one extra tube per level. Restarting is always free.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Marblume has no accounts. To move your progress to a new phone, open Settings → Back up progress, keep the code it shows, then on the new device open Settings → Restore from a code and paste it. Deleting the game or clearing its data removes your progress.

What is a frozen tube?

From World 2, some levels start with one spare tube frozen. The ice shows a colour: finish a tube of that colour and the ice melts, giving you an extra empty tube. Hint knows about frozen tubes too.

How do I start again from level 1?

Open Settings (the gear icon) → Reset progress. This erases levels, stars, seeds, your collection and profile on that device.

How do I get seeds and unlock items?

You earn seeds by playing: 4 per star the first time you finish a level, 50 every tenth level, 20 for each Daily Bloom, 200 for finishing every Daily Bloom in a month, and 25 to 500 for each badge. Spend them in the Shop. Some special items are earned through achievements instead.

Sound, vibration or animations are too much.

Open Settings and switch off Sound, Haptics, or turn on Reduce motion. You will also find Relaxed play (an extra empty tube), Appearance (light or dark), Text size, Language, colour-blind symbols and a left-handed toolbar.

What does “Share anonymous game stats” send?

Only if you said yes: for each level, the level number, moves, par, stars, hints, extra tubes, undos, time and whether you solved it, with a random code made by the app — never your name, email or location. Switch it off or delete it any time in Settings → Privacy.
