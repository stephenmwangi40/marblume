Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: your progress stays on your device. If you agree, the game also sends anonymous game results so we can balance the puzzles — never your name, email, contacts or location. You can switch this off and delete it at any time.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, seeds, collection, daily results, statistics, profile (the optional name, avatar and ring colour) and settings. Nobody else can see them. Deleting the game removes them, and Settings → Reset progress erases them any time. A backup code is only made when you ask for one, and only you keep it.

Anonymous game stats (optional)

Only if you choose “Share”: for each level you finish or leave, the level number, mode, moves, par, stars, hints, extra tubes, undos, time taken and whether you solved it, together with a random code made by the app, the app version, the platform and your language. It is not linked to you, not used for ads and never sold. We use it only to make levels fairer and fix problems. Settings → Delete my shared stats removes it from our server; it is also deleted after 24 months.

Short surveys (optional)

Sometimes a short question appears on the Home screen, such as how much you enjoy the game. Answering is optional and gives no rewards. We store your answer, and the random code only if you share stats.

Ads

The Android and iPhone apps can show short videos from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one for a hint, an extra tube or seeds. There are never ads between levels, and the web version has none. Google uses your device’s advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it you are asked first, Settings → Ad choices changes your answer, and on iPhone you choose whether apps may track you. Ads are limited to content suitable for everyone.

Contacting us

If you write to us from our website's Support page, we receive only what you send, use it only to reply, and delete it 12 months after the last message.

Permissions

Internet, for the optional stats, surveys and videos; vibration, for optional haptics; and, in the phone apps, the advertising ID used by Google’s ads.

Children

Marblume is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content for general audiences, and anonymous stats stay off unless someone switches them on.
